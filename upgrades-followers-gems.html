Upgrades, followers and gems

A reference for the numbers behind WHAT HAPPENED clicker: what each upgrade costs and earns, what Golden What Happeneds buy, how followers and lootboxes work, and where gems come from.

There is no real-money purchase anywhere in the game. Gems, Golden What Happeneds, followers, lootboxes and skins are all earned by playing. Nothing can be bought with money, and the casino only uses in-game currencies.

The price of each new upgrade jumps more than its income does, so every new upgrade takes a bit longer to reach than the last. Evil Somalian Pirate and O'algo are meant for later runs: out of reach in a first run, and quicker to reach as your Divine upgrades grow.

Specials unlock at set levels of each upgrade (OP mouse: 10, 25, 50, 100, 150, 200, 250, 300) and multiply that upgrade, your click power or your whole income. Milestones from level 500 multiply an upgrade's output again: ×2 every 25 levels, ×4 every 100 and ×10 every 1,000.

Followers

Followers are collectable creatures, managed on the Followers page. Up to your number of team slots can be on your team at once, and each team member gives you bonuses. You start with 2 slots and can buy up to 8 with Golden (2, 5, 10, 20, 40 and 80 Golden for slots 3 to 8). Followers are stored separately from your main save, so prestige never touches them.

Families

  • 🌿 Swamp: keep What Happeneds flowing while you idle (CPS and offline earnings).
  • 🔥 Ember: click power and critical damage.
  • ❄️ Frost: big offline earnings.
  • ⚡ Storm: critical chance.
  • 🌌 Void: more Golden every prestige.
  • 👾 Glitch: finds gems.
  • 💪 Chad: huge critical hits.
  • 🥖 Bakery: boosts to CPS and clicks.

There are six rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary and Mythic. Rarer followers give bigger bonuses and can reach higher levels. Shiny followers give 25% more.

Levelling and breeding

Feed followers to each other to level them up. Two followers can also breed an egg (each follower can breed 3 times). Eggs hatch in the nursery, up to 3 at a time. Hatching takes the two parents' times added together: two Commons hatch in 5 minutes, two Mythics in 12 hours.

Lootboxes, charms and items

Lootboxes appear on the game screen every 5 to 10 minutes while the game is open in front of you, and stay for 14 seconds. They go into your Items (🎒). Three boxes of one kind merge into one of the next kind up.

  • Charms give a permanent bonus while equipped. You start with 3 charm slots and can unlock a 4th and 5th for 40 and 120 gems. Each extra copy of a charm adds a star, up to 5 stars (3× the bonus). A copy past 5 stars turns into seconds of CPS (Common and Uncommon) or gems (Rare and up).
  • Consumables are used once: timed boosts, or instant What Happeneds, gems or Golden.
  • A lure (15 gems) makes lootboxes appear 3× as often for 15 minutes.

Items are stored separately from your main save, so prestige never touches them.

Gems

Gems are meant to be rare. Where they come from:

  • Clicking: each click has a 1 in 2,500 chance. Glitch followers, gem charms and the Hall of Gods raise it.
  • Lootboxes: Golden Chests and better can contain gems, and so can the Gem Pouch (2) and Gem Geode (10) consumables.
  • Extra charm copies past 5 stars, for Rare charms and up.
  • The Wilds: bosses, elites, ruby veins and the Trader's weekly exchange, up to 5 gems a day.
  • Clan events, codes and wins in the casino when you bet gems.

What they buy: skins, backgrounds and music in the Shop, lootboxes in the Shop's Treasury, the 4th and 5th charm slots, and the lure. Selling items gives back only a tiny fraction of a gem, so buying boxes to sell what's inside always loses gems.

Gems can't be bought with money. If you see someone offering to sell gems or accounts, it isn't from this game.
