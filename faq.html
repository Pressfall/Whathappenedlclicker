Frequently asked questions

Playing

Is the game free? Can I pay for anything?
It's free, and there is nothing to pay for. There are no real-money purchases: gems, Golden What Happeneds, followers, lootboxes and skins are all earned by playing. The site shows ads only if you accept them (see ads and cookies).
Does the game keep earning when I close it?
Yes. When you come back you're paid for the time you were away, up to 24 hours, at your normal CPS (temporary boosts don't count for that time).
When can I prestige, and should I?
Prestige unlocks when CR lurker reaches level 150. It resets your run and gives you one Golden What Happened for every 1,500 upgrade levels you own. Each Golden you hold adds +10% CPS and click power, and Golden also buy permanent Divine upgrades, so later runs go much faster. See prestige.
Why can't I join a clan?
Clans unlock after your first prestige. Until then you can look around the clan page but not join or create one.
Can I play on my phone?
The game runs in the browser, so it works on phones and tablets as well as computers. The Wilds shows on-screen controls on touch screens, and its Settings have an Assisted swings option for touch screens and trackpads.
Is the casino real gambling?
No. The casino (blackjack, slots, the rocket and a free daily spin) only uses What Happeneds, gems or Golden from your save. No money goes in or comes out.

Saves

Where is my progress saved?
In this browser, on this device. The game saves automatically. Clearing your browser's site data, using a private window, or opening the game in another browser starts a new game, so it's worth keeping a backup.
How do I move my progress to another device or browser?
Two ways. Download a save file in Settings → Save backup, then use Import save file… on the other browser. Or save to an account (Settings → Account, or the Account page): sign in with Google or email on both devices and your progress follows you.
I lost my progress. Can it be recovered?
If you saved to an account, sign in again and it comes back. If you downloaded a save file, import it. Otherwise the save only existed in the browser it was made in, so nobody else has a copy to restore. If the game ever finds a save it can't read, it keeps that save to one side instead of overwriting it.
What does prestige keep?
Your Golden What Happeneds, Divine upgrades, gems, clan points, shop purchases and settings. Followers, items and your progress in the Wilds are saved separately and are never reset by a prestige.

The Wilds

The Wilds says it's full, or online play isn't available. What can I do?
Online play runs on a free plan that limits how many people can be connected at once and how much data can be sent in a month. When it's busy, the 🌐 menu says so and you keep playing offline: monsters, crafting, quests and bosses all work offline. Try again later.
Does the Wilds affect my clicker game?
Yes. It can send gems (up to 5 a day), Golden What Happeneds (up to 1 a day) and crafted lootboxes to the clicker, and the Hall of Gods statues give the clicker permanent bonuses. A prestige in the clicker never resets the Wilds. See the Wilds guide.
Can other players attack me?
Outside town, when you're playing online, yes. Town is always safe, and you're protected for a few seconds after arriving somewhere. If you don't want to meet anyone, choose to play offline in the 🌐 menu.

Leaderboard and chat

How does the leaderboard work? Is it checked?
Press Submit score in the game to send your current number of What Happeneds, with your username and equipped title. This month shows the best score each name has submitted since the 1st of the current month (the month changes at 00:00 UTC, which may be a different time where you are); All time shows the best each name has ever submitted. A name is whatever the player typed, not an account. Scores come from players' own browsers and are not checked, so treat the board as just for fun.
How does the swear filter work?
Safe mode hides swear words in every chat: the game's global and clan chat, the clan page, and the Wilds. It's on unless you turn it off, and you can switch it back on at any time. It only changes what your own screen shows. It doesn't remove messages for anyone else, and it only knows a fixed list of English words.
What should I use as a username?
Anything that isn't your real name or other personal details. Usernames, chat messages and leaderboard entries are visible to everyone.

Ads, privacy and contact

Why does the site ask about ads and cookies?
The game page can show an ad banner from Google AdSense, and visits to it can be counted with Google Analytics. Neither loads unless you choose Accept. Decline works just as well and doesn't change the game. To change your choice, open Settings → Help & privacy → Ad & cookie choices. The privacy policy has the details.
How do I report a bug or get in touch?
Join the game's Discord: discord.gg/Puz4EmbdXS. The same invite is in the game under Settings. For privacy requests, see the privacy policy.
