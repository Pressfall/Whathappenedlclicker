Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026

Overview

This privacy policy explains what information WHAT HAPPENED clicker collects and how it is used. The site stores game state locally (via localStorage), and optionally posts public leaderboard entries to a leaderboard server when enabled. This page focuses on how data related to the game's features is handled.

Data Controller & Contact

Data controller: the operator of the WHAT HAPPENED clicker website. For privacy questions or to exercise your rights (access, deletion, correction, portability, restriction), contact: whathappenedclicker@gmail.com.

Information We Collect

  • Local save data: Your game progress (counts, levels, settings, and divine/golden state) is stored in your browser's localStorage. This data stays on your device unless you sign in to an account (see below) or intentionally share it. You can also download your progress as a file (Settings → Save backup) and import it again; that file is created on your device and goes nowhere unless you send it.
  • Accounts and cloud saves (optional): If you create an account or sign in, we store your email address (and, for Google sign-in, your Google account name and profile picture) with Google Firebase Authentication, and a copy of your game progress, the time it was saved and a short device description such as "Chrome on Windows" in Google Cloud Firestore. Passwords are handled by Firebase Authentication; the game never sees or stores them.
  • Leaderboard entries: When you save a username and submit a score to the leaderboard, the leaderboard stores the username, your equipped title, your score (a number) and a timestamp, and shows them to everyone. Scores come from players' own browsers and are not verified. If you run the optional leaderboard server, that server will persist these entries.
  • Server logs & metadata: If you submit leaderboard data to a hosted leaderboard server (for example, Firestore/Firestore REST endpoints), that server may also log IP addresses, request metadata, and other technical information necessary to operate and secure the service.
  • Chat, clans and profiles: If you chat, join a clan or play with a username, that username, your messages, your clan membership and some game numbers (your What Happeneds, Golden, clan points and equipped title) are stored in Google Cloud Firestore and are visible to other players. Do not use your real name or other personal details as a username.
  • Third-party services: Ads (Google AdSense) and visit counting (Google Analytics) are loaded only after you choose Accept in the ad and cookie notice (see Advertising below). Google Fonts (the game's text fonts) and Google Firebase (accounts, cloud saves, chat, clans and the leaderboard) are used to run the site, so your browser contacts Google servers for them and Google receives your IP address and ordinary request data. These services may use cookies or similar storage as described in their own policies.

How We Use Information

  • Store and restore your game progress in localStorage for a better experience.
  • If you have an account: sign you in, save your progress to it, and load it on your other devices. Your cloud save can only be read or changed while signed in to your account.
  • Publish leaderboard entries (username, score, timestamp) only when you submit them intentionally via the leaderboard UI.
  • Use third-party content or services that may use cookies or other browser storage to provide or measure their features.

Legal Basis (for EU/EEA users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), we rely on the following lawful bases for processing personal data:

  • Performance of a contract / provision of service: storing and restoring your game progress in localStorage and enabling game functionality.
  • Legitimate interests: operating and securing leaderboard services and preventing abuse.
  • Consent: ads and visit counting (Google AdSense, including personalized ads, and Google Analytics) are loaded only if you accept them. You can withdraw consent at any time (see Advertising); declining does not affect the game.

Advertising

The site can show ads through Google AdSense (publisher ID ca-pub-7259344384263515) on the game, achievements, casino and leaderboard pages, and counts visits to the game page with Google Analytics (through Firebase). Neither is loaded until you choose Accept in the notice shown on your first visit. If you choose Decline, or have not chosen yet, no AdSense or Analytics code is loaded, your browser does not contact Google for them, and no space is kept for ads. Accept and Decline are offered equally.

If you accept, Google may use cookies and other identifiers to provide, personalize and measure ads and to count visits. The use of AdSense and Analytics on this site is subject to Google's policies and privacy practices. For details, see:

  • Google Ads technologies
  • Google Privacy & Terms

Changing your choice

In the game, open Settings → Help & privacy → Ad & cookie choices. On the other pages, use the Ad & cookie choices link at the bottom of the page. Choosing Decline stops new ad and Analytics requests; ads already on the page go away when you reload it. Cookies that Google already set stay in your browser until you delete them in your browser's settings. Your choice is stored in your browser (localStorage, key wh_ad_consent); if you accepted in the earlier notice (key wh_cookie_consent), that still counts until you change it.

Ad personalization & opt-out

If you want to opt out of ad personalization by Google, visit Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of many third-party ad networks via the Network Advertising Initiative (NAI) or the Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA).

Cookies and Local Storage

The site itself uses localStorage to save your game, your settings and your ad choice. The game needs these to work and they are not shared with advertisers. We do not set our own cookies. If you accept, Google AdSense and Google Analytics may set cookies or similar storage; Google Fonts and Firebase may also store small amounts of data, each according to its own policy.

Leaderboard Server

When you submit a score to the public leaderboard, only the username (up to 32 characters), your equipped title, your score (a number), and a timestamp are sent to the configured leaderboard API endpoint. If you run your own local leaderboard server, that server's operator controls how the data is stored and processed. The client will not automatically share your local save file—only the explicit leaderboard submission is sent.

Third-party hosting: the default leaderboard implementation may use Google Firebase / Firestore. See Firebase privacy information: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy. The leaderboard server operator may collect additional data such as IP addresses or request logs necessary for security and abuse prevention.

Data Retention

Local save data remains on your device until you delete it (via the game's "Clear Save" control or by clearing localStorage). Leaderboard entries are retained on the leaderboard server according to that server's policy.

Accounts: your account and cloud save are kept until you delete them. You can delete your account at any time on the Account page (Security → Delete account), which removes the account and its cloud save immediately; the progress on your device is not affected.

Suggested default retention: if you operate a leaderboard server, consider retaining leaderboard entries for a reasonable period (for example, 12 months) unless you are required to keep them longer. Provide users with the ability to request deletion sooner.

  • To control ads, visit counting and cookies, use Ad & cookie choices (see Advertising), your browser controls, and the links above for Google Ads Settings and opt-out resources.
