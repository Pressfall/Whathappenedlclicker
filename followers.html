Breeding

Pick two followers to make a new one based on its parents. The parents are kept.

  • Each follower can breed 3 times. Breeding costs Golden, more for rarer parents.
  • Eggs take time to hatch: each parent adds 2.5 min (Common), 7.5 min, 20 min, 1 h, 3 h or 6 h (Mythic). A parent can only be in one egg at a time.
  • The baby's rarity is the average of its parents' rarities, with a chance to come out one rarity higher. Two Legendaries are the main way to get a Mythic.
  • Same-species pairs (and higher-level parents) have better upgrade odds. Same-family pairs keep the family.
  • Babies get a bloodline bonus of +3% to their buffs per generation (up to +30%), and shiny parents raise the shiny chance.
