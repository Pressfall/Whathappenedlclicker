About WHAT HAPPENED clicker

WHAT HAPPENED clicker is a free idle and clicker game that you play in your web browser. There's nothing to install, no sign-up needed, and nothing to buy.

What's in the game

  • The clicker: click, buy 29 upgrades and their specials, and prestige for Golden What Happeneds that power up every later run. How to play
  • The Wilds: a top-down adventure where you swing weapons with the mouse, gather and craft your own gear, choose a class, fight bosses and, if you like, play with others online. The Wilds guide
  • Followers, lootboxes and charms that add bonuses, plus achievements and titles. Upgrades, followers and gems
  • Clans, a global chat and a public leaderboard.
  • A casino that only uses in-game currencies.

How it's made

The game is written in plain HTML, CSS and JavaScript and runs entirely in your browser. Your progress is saved there too. Google Firebase runs the optional online parts: accounts and cloud saves, chat, clans, the leaderboard, and playing together in the Wilds.

How it's paid for

The game is free. The game page can show an ad banner from Google AdSense, but only if you choose Accept in the ad and cookie notice. Declining doesn't change anything in the game. There are no real-money purchases: everything in the game is earned by playing. Online play in the Wilds runs on Firebase's free plan, which is why it can sometimes be full.

Fair play

Everything runs in players' own browsers, so the leaderboard can't check scores and is just for fun. Chat has a swear filter that's on unless you turn it off. In the Wilds, each player's page checks the hits it receives, and rewards sent to the clicker are capped each day. More on how this works is in the FAQ.

Contact

The best way to reach the people behind the game, report a bug or suggest something is the game's Discord: discord.gg/Puz4EmbdXS. The same invite is in the game under Settings → Join our Discord.
