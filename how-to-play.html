How to play

WHAT HAPPENED clicker is a free idle game that runs in your browser. You click to earn What Happeneds, spend them on upgrades that earn more for you, and eventually reset your run for Golden What Happeneds that make every later run faster. This page walks through a first run from the very first click.

Your first few minutes

A new game shows a small guide card above the main image with five steps. It ticks them off as you go and disappears once you know your way around:

  1. Click the image to earn What Happeneds. How much each click gives is your click power, shown in the stats.
  2. Buy your first OP mouse. It costs 100 What Happeneds and adds click power, so every click after it is worth more.
  3. Buy a Somalian Pirate (500). It earns for you every second, even when you aren't clicking. Income per second is your CPS.
  4. Raise OP mouse to level 10. That unlocks its first special.
  5. Open Specials and buy one. Specials are one-off purchases that multiply an upgrade or your whole income.

After that the card points you at the next special you are closest to and the next upgrade you haven't bought yet. You can close it whenever you like.

Upgrades and buying in bulk

The upgrades are down the left side of the game. There are 29 of them. The first, OP mouse, adds click power; every other one adds CPS. Each level costs a little more than the last: 10% more for the first ten upgrades and 15% more from the eleventh on. Hover over an upgrade to see what one level gives you right now.

The buttons above the list (1, 5, 10, 25, 50, 100 and Max) choose how many levels one press buys. Max buys as many as you can afford. The button shows the total price before you buy.

Each new upgrade takes longer to reach than the one before it, so a new one is a goal rather than something you unlock every few seconds. The full list, with prices, is on the upgrades, followers and gems page.

Specials and milestones

Specials unlock when an upgrade reaches certain levels. For OP mouse they unlock at levels 10, 25, 50, 100, 150, 200, 250 and 300. Some boost only their own upgrade, some boost click power, and some multiply nearly all of your CPS. The ones that multiply everything cost a lot more, so they arrive spread out over a run instead of all at once.

Milestones start at level 500 of any upgrade. From there, every 25 levels doubles what that upgrade gives, every 100 levels multiplies it by 4, and every 1,000 levels by 10. The upgrade's tooltip shows the next milestone.

Earning while you're away

When you come back, the game pays you for the time you were gone, up to 24 hours, at your normal CPS. Short boosts that were running (casino boosts, clan shop buffs, timed items) don't count for offline time, but permanent bonuses from followers, charms and clan level do, and some of them raise offline earnings specifically.

Lootboxes, gems and items

Every so often a lootbox appears somewhere on the screen. Your first comes after a minute and a half to two and a half minutes, then one every 5 to 10 minutes while the game is open in front of you. Each stays for 14 seconds, so click it quickly. Lootboxes go into your Items (🎒). Open them for charms (permanent bonuses while equipped) and consumables (timed boosts and instant rewards), or merge three of one kind into one better box.

Gems are the rare currency. Each click has a 1 in 2,500 chance to find one, and better lootboxes can contain them. Spend them in the Shop on skins, backgrounds and music, or on extra lootboxes and charm slots. Gems can't be bought with money. See where gems come from.

Prestige and Golden What Happeneds

Prestige unlocks once CR lurker (upgrade 12) reaches level 150. Prestiging ends your run: your What Happeneds, upgrades and specials go back to the start, and in return you get Golden What Happeneds, one for every 1,500 upgrade levels you own in total. The Prestige button shows how many you would get now and roughly how many more levels you need for the next.

Golden What Happeneds help in two ways:

  • Holding them: each Golden you hold adds +10% to your CPS and click power.
  • Spending them in the Divine menu on permanent upgrades (for example much more CPS, much more click power, critical clicks, cheaper upgrades, a head start on each run, or more Golden from each prestige), or on followers. Spending lowers the holding bonus, so it's a trade.

A prestige keeps your Golden, Divine upgrades, gems, clan points, shop purchases and settings. Followers, items and your progress in the Wilds are stored separately and are never touched by it.

Your first prestige takes a while: roughly two hours of steady play. Later runs go a lot faster as your Golden and Divine upgrades add up. Your first prestige also unlocks clans.

Everything else to do

  • Followers (🦎): collectable creatures bought in packs with Golden What Happeneds. Put them on your team for bonuses such as CPS, click power, crits, offline earnings, gems or extra Golden. You can level them up and breed them for eggs. See followers.
  • The Wilds (⚔️): a separate top-down adventure with its own currency, rubies. You fight with weapons you swing using the mouse, gather, and craft. It can send gems, Golden and lootboxes back to the clicker. See the Wilds guide.
  • Achievements (🎖️): goals across the whole game, each with a title you can equip in Settings and show on the leaderboard and in chat.
  • Casino (🎰): blackjack, slots, a rocket game and a free daily spin. You bet What Happeneds, gems or Golden from your save; there is no real money involved.
  • Clans (🛡️): unlock after your first prestige. Join or start a clan, check in daily, take part in clan events and spend clan points in the clan shop.
  • Leaderboard (🏆): submit your score to the public board. Scores come from players' own browsers and aren't checked, so treat it as just for fun.
  • Chat: the chat panel in the game has a global chat and, once you are in one, your clan's chat. A swear filter (Safe mode) is on unless you turn it off.
  • Codes: if you have a code, enter it in Settings → Codes and press Redeem.

Keeping your progress safe

Your progress is saved automatically in this browser. Clearing your browser's site data, using a private window, or switching to another browser or device starts a new game. Two ways to keep it safe:

  • Download a save file: Settings → Save backup → Download save file. Import it later with Import save file…, on this browser or another one.
  • Save to an account: Settings → Account, or the Account page. Sign in with Google or email and your progress is backed up and follows you to any device.
